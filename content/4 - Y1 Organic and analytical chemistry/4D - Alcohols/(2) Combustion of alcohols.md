Specification reference: [[OCR A Chemistry Specification#Reactions of alcohols|Reactions of alcohols]]

All alcohols can undergo combustion reacts. As with [[(2) Reactions of alkanes|alkanes]], they must be vapours first. Short alcohols can evaporate easily, and hence will burn easily. They also need less oxygen per mole, so they are likely to burn completely. The longer the alcohol, the less volatile and so the less flammable.

Complete combustion:
$$
\ce{
C_{2}H_{5}OH + 3O_{2} -> 2CO_{2} + 3H_{2}O
}
$$
Incomplete combustion:
$$
\ce{
C_{2}H_{5}OH + 2 \frac{1}{2}O_{2} -> 2CO + 3H_{2}O
}
$$
