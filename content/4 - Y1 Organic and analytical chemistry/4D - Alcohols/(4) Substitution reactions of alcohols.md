Specification reference: [[OCR A Chemistry Specification#Reactions of alcohols|Reactions of alcohols]]

Alcohols can be converted to [[(1) Introduction to haloalkanes|haloalkanes]] by [[(2) Nucleophilic substitution|nucleophilic substitution]]. The general equation below includes the halide ion as X:
$$\ce{
alcohol + NaX + H_{2}SO_{4} -> haloalkane + NaHSO_{4} + H_{2}O
}$$
In this reaction, the sodium halide and sulfuric acid react together to produce a hydrogen halide in situ. This occurs under reflux conditions. The hydrogen halide then reacts with the alcohol to form a haloalkane:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\ce{
Step 1: NaX + H_{2}SO_{4} -> HX + NaHSO_{4}
} \\[3mm]
\ce{Step 2: HX + alcohol -> H_{2}O + haloalkane}
\end{aligned}$$
