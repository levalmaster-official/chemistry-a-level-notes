Specification reference: [[OCR A Chemistry Specification#4.2.2 Haloalkanes|4.2.2 Haloalkanes]]

Haloalkanes contain $\ce{F, Cl, Br,}$ or $\ce{I}$ atoms. They are [[(1) Naming organic molecules#Naming alkanes|named]] like branches, using the prefixes fluoro, chloro, bromo, and iodo respectively. The halogen is higher in priority than alkyl groups, so the chain is numbered so that the halogen has the lowest number.

Haloalkanes can be **classified**, based of the the number of hydrogens bonded to the carbon that the halogen is bonded to. This is similar to the classification of [[(5) Carbocation stability#Carbocation classification|carbocations]].

| Primary                                                                      | Secondary                                                                    | Tertiary                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Haloalkane is on a carbon attached to one alkyl group and two hydrogen atoms | Haloalkane is on a carbon attached to two alkyl groups and one hydrogen atom | Haloalkane is on a carbon attached to three alkyl groups and no hydrogen atoms |
