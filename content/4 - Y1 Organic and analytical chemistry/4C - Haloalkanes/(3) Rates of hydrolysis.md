#### Bond enthalpies of C-X bonds

| Bond                               | C-F | C-Cl | C-Br | C-I |
| ---------------------------------- | --- | ---- | ---- | --- |
| Bond enthalpy / $\ce{kJ mol}^{-1}$ | 467 | 340  | 280  | 240 |

$\ce{C-F}$ bonds are stronger than [[(2) Reactions of alkanes|C-H bonds]]; fluoroalkanes do not undergo [[(2) Nucleophilic substitution|nucleophilic substitution]]. [[(1) Alkanes|Alkanes]] do not undergo nucleophilic substitution as $\ce{C-H}$ bonds are not polar.

>[!QUESTION] A student decides to prepare butan-1-ol by the alkaline hydrolysis of 1-chlorobutane. Suggest, with reasons, any change in the conditions from those used in the alkaline hydrolysis of 1-iodobutane given above.
>A C-Cl bond is stronger (higher bond enthalpy) than the C-I bond, so 1-iodobutane will undergo hydrolysis reactions much more readily compared to 1-chlorobutane. Therefore, the alkaline hydrolysis of 1-chlorobutane will require reflux for a longer time (more than 20 minutes) than 1-iodobutane to prepare a sample of butan-1-ol
