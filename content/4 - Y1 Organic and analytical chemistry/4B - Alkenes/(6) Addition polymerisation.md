Specification reference: [[OCR A Chemistry Specification#Polymers from alkenes|Polymers from alkenes]]

Addition polymerisation is the formation of a very long molecular chain, by repeated addition reactions of many unsaturated alkene molecules (monomers)

A monomer is the repeating unit that joins with addition reactions to form a polymer.

![[Pasted image 20250326011712.png]]
When writing an equation for the formation of a polymer, $n$ must be used to show lots of monomers joining to make the polymer.
