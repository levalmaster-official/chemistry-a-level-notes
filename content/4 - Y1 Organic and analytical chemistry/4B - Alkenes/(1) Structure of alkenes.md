Specification reference: [[OCR A Chemistry Specification#Properties of alkenes|Properties of alkenes]]

Alkenes are unsaturated hydrocarbons. They contain the functional group C=C. Their carbon-carbon double bonds are made up of two parts, a [[(6) Sigma and pi bonds#Sigma bonds|sigma]] bond and a [[(6) Sigma and pi bonds#Pi bonds|pi]] bond. There is restricted rotation of the $\pi$-bond.

Alkenes have a [[(3) Shapes of molecules|trigonal planar]] shape around each carbon atom due to the double bond.

>[!EXAMPLE] Describe and explain, using VSEPR, the shape around each C around the C=C in an alkane.
>There are three bonding pairs of electrons (and no lone pairs) around each central carbon, that repel as far apart as possible. This results in bond angles of 120º and a trigonal planar shape.
