There are two different types of covalent bond fission: homolytic fission and heterolytic fission.

#### Homolytic fission
Homolytic fission occurs when each bonded atom receives one electron from the bonded pair, forming two radicals (that is, a species with an unpaired electron).
$$\ce{Br-Br -> Br^{.} + Br^{.}}$$

#### Heterolytic fission
Heterolytic fission occurs when one bonding atom receives **both** electrons from the bonded pair.
$$\ce{Br-Br -> Br- + Br+}$$
In chemical mechanisms, full-headed <span style='color:indianred;'>curly arrows</span> are often used (usually in red) to show **the movement of a pair of electrons** into or out of bonds and lone pairs.
