Specification reference: [[OCR A Chemistry Specification#Hess' law and enthalpy cycles|Hess' law and enthalpy cycles]]

Hess cycles can be used to indirectly calculate the [[(1) Enthalpy changes|enthalpy change]] of a reaction using standard enthalpy changes of other reactions of the reactants and products. They rely on Hess' law:

>[!NOTE] Hess's law
>The enthalpy change for any chemical reaction is independent of the intermediate stages so long as the initial and final conditions are the same for each route
