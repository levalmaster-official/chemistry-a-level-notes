Specification reference: [[OCR A Chemistry Specification#Bond enthalpy|Bond enthalpy]]

Bond breaking is [[(1) Enthalpy changes|endothermic]] and bond making is exothermic. A reaction is exothermic if less or more energy is required to break bonds than is released when new bonds are made.

>[!QUOTE] Bond enthalpy
>The **average** enthalpy change when 1 mole of a particular type of [[(2) Covalent bonding#Covalent bonding|covalent bond]] in a molecule is broken [[(5) Reaction mechanisms#Homolytic fission|homolytically]] in the **gas** phase

Bond enthalpies are slightly different in different molecules, so we tend to use averages to simplify calculations and make predictions.

When we experimentally find bond enthalpies, they may be different to the average bond enthalpy because:
1. The reactants may not be in the gas phase
2. The value provided is an average/ is non-specific
