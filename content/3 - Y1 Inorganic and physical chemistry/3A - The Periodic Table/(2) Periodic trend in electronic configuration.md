### Periodic table
The periodic table may be divided into 4 blocks determined by highest energy level orbitals filled by  the electrons of atoms of those elements. These blocks are as follows (where $n$ is the row number):
- s-block: two columns on the left, where the $n s$ orbitals are filled 
- p-block: six columns on the right, where the $np$ orbital are filled
- d-block: ten columns in the middle, where the $(n-1)d$ orbitals are filled
- f-block: fourteen columns at the bottom, where the $(n-2)f$ orbitals are filled

>[!TLDR] Summary
