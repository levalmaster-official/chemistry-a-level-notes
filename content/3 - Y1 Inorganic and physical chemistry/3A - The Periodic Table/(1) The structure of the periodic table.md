Specification reference: [[OCR A Chemistry Specification#The structure of the periodic table|The structure of the periodic table]]

The periodic table arranges elements from left to right in order of increasing atomic number. Vertical columns called groups have similar chemical properties, and atoms of these elements share the [[(2) Periodic trend in electronic configuration|same number]] of outer-shell electrons. Horizontal periods show [[(4) Structure and melting point|repeating trends]] in physical and chemical properties, which is known as periodicity. 

>[!SUMMARY] Periodicity
>The repeating trend in properties of the elements across each period of the periodic table.
