Specification reference: [[OCR A Chemistry Specification#Catalysts|Catalysts]]

A catalyst is a chemical that will increase the rate of a chemical reaction without being used up by the overall reaction. There are two types of catalysts:
- Homogenous catalysts are in the **same** physical state as the reactants
- Heterogenous catalysts are in a **different** physical state to the reactants

Catalysts work by providing an alternative reaction pathway with a lower activation energy. This means particles are more likely to exceed the activation energy on collision, so the reaction rate increases.
